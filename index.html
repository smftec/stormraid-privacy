StormRaid Pro Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026 · App: com.stormraid.pro · 雷电空战专业版

TL;DR: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

1. Data We Collect — None

StormRaid Pro does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All game progress (gold, gems, aircraft, upgrades, statistics, settings) is saved locally on your device using the app's private storage. This data never leaves your device unless you use Android's own backup service, which is controlled by your device settings and Google account, not by us.

2. No Advertising or Analytics

The app contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics trackers, and no third-party data collection components of any kind.

3. In-App Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (gem packs) are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment information. Purchases can be restored anytime via Shop → Restore purchases. Google's handling of payment data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Permissions

  • Vibration — haptic feedback when you take damage or trigger abilities. Can be disabled in Settings.
  • Internet / Billing — used only by Google Play Billing to process optional purchases.

No location, contacts, camera, microphone, or storage permissions are requested.

5. Children

The game is rated for everyone (fantasy violence, no blood). We do not knowingly collect any data from users of any age, including children under 13.

6. Data Deletion

Uninstalling the app removes all local data. You can also reset progress in-game via Settings → Reset progress.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated with app updates. The latest version is always available in-game under Settings → Privacy Policy.

8. Contact

Questions? Contact us at the support email listed on the Google Play store listing.

隐私政策（简体中文）

摘要：我们不收集、存储或传输任何个人数据，所有数据仅保存在您的设备上。

1. 我们收集的数据 —— 无

《雷电空战专业版》不收集、存储或传输任何个人数据。所有游戏进度（金币、钻石、战机、强化、统计、设置）均通过应用私有存储保存在您的设备本地。除非您使用 Android 系统自带的备份服务（由您的设备设置与 Google 账号控制，与我们无关），这些数据不会离开您的设备。

2. 无广告与分析

本应用不包含广告 SDK、数据统计分析器或任何第三方数据收集组件。

3. 应用内购买

可选内购（钻石礼包）完全由 Google Play 处理，我们无法接触您的支付信息。您可随时在「商店 → 恢复购买」找回购买。Google 对支付数据的处理遵循 Google 隐私政策。

4. 权限说明

  • 振动——受击或触发技能时的触觉反馈，可在设置中关闭。
  • 网络 / 结算——仅由 Google Play 结算服务用于处理可选购买。

本应用不申请定位、通讯录、相机、麦克风或存储权限。

5. 儿童隐私

游戏分级适合所有年龄段（幻想暴力、无血腥）。我们不会有意收集任何年龄用户（包括 13 岁以下儿童）的数据。

6. 数据删除

卸载应用即删除全部本地数据。您也可以在游戏内「设置 → 重置游戏进度」清除进度。

7. 政策变更

本政策可能随应用更新而更新。最新版本始终可在游戏内「设置 → 隐私政策」查看。

8. 联系我们

如有疑问，请通过 Google Play 商店页面列出的支持邮箱联系我们。
